13. Troubleshooting & FAQ

AI generation is slow or fails

Symptom: Clicking Generate produces no output, a timeout error, or a generic “AI generation failed” message.

Causes and fixes: - The AI service can take 15–30 seconds for longer workflows (Lesson Drafter in particular). Wait at least 30 seconds before concluding it has failed. - Check your AI credit balance in Settings. If you have 0 credits, generation will be blocked immediately. - If generation consistently fails, the AI service may be temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few minutes. - Very long file content (over 50,000 characters) will be rejected. Split large files into multiple lessons.

“A course with this title already exists” error

Symptom: Creating a course returns a duplicate title error.

Fix: Course slugs must be unique per account. Choose a different title, or append a year or version number (e.g. “Introduction to Python 2026”).

GitHub commit fails

Symptom: Clicking “Commit to GitHub” shows an error.

Causes and fixes: - Your GitHub connection may have expired. Go to Settings, disconnect, and reconnect. - The repository may have been deleted manually on GitHub. CourseFoundry will attempt to recreate it on the next commit. - GitHub’s API may be temporarily unavailable. Check githubstatus.com and retry. - If you recently revoked CourseFoundry’s GitHub access from your GitHub account settings, reconnect from Settings.

Published course not appearing on /learn

Symptom: You published a course but it doesn’t appear in the catalog.

Causes and fixes: - Allow up to 30 seconds after publishing for the catalog to update. Refresh the page. - Confirm the course is showing as Published (green badge) on your dashboard. If it shows Draft, the publish action may not have saved — click the globe icon again. - If the course appears on your dashboard as Published but is missing from the catalog, try unpublishing and republishing.

Lessons not appearing in the right order

Symptom: The lesson list on the public course page doesn’t match what you expect.

Fix: The sidebar order on the rendered site is determined by _quarto.yml. CourseFoundry regenerates this file automatically when you show or hide folders, but if you have manually edited _quarto.yml the auto-regeneration will overwrite your changes the next time you toggle visibility. To fine-tune ordering, open _quarto.yml in the editor — just be aware that a visibility toggle will regenerate it. File and folder names with a numeric prefix (e.g. 01-, 02-) sort alphabetically and therefore control the order reliably.

How do I reset my AI credits?

Credits reset automatically on the 1st of each month. You cannot manually reset them. If you need more credits before the reset, upgrade your plan or wait for the monthly reset.

Can I export my course as SCORM?

Yes. Open your course in the editor, click the LMS Export tab in the right panel, then click Download SCORM Package. The package can be uploaded to any SCORM-compliant LMS (Moodle, Blackboard, Brightspace, etc.).

Note: All course content must be visible (not hidden) before downloading a SCORM package. If any folders are hidden via the Eye toggle, an amber warning appears and the download button is replaced with a prompt to unhide all content first.

PDF export is planned for a future release. Your source files are standard Quarto Markdown — if you have GitHub integration enabled, you can clone your repository and run quarto render locally to produce any output format Quarto supports.

How do I release content week by week (staged publishing)?

Use the Eye toggle in the file tree. Hover over a week or module folder and click the Eye icon to hide it. Hidden folders are excluded from the published sidebar — students cannot navigate to them even if they know the URL.

Each week before you want content to go live: 1. Hover the folder in the file tree and click the EyeOff icon to reveal it 2. The sidebar is regenerated automatically 3. Commit and push — GitHub Actions re-renders the site and the new week appears for students

Is my content private?

Yes. All courses are private by default (Draft status). Only you can see your draft courses — not other CourseFoundry users, not the public. Your content is only made public if you explicitly click the Publish button.

Once published, the course is publicly accessible without login. Unpublishing immediately reverts the course to private.

Can I collaborate with other authors on the same course?

Team collaboration is planned for the Enterprise plan (Phase 17). Currently, each course belongs to one account. As a workaround, you can share a GitHub repository with a collaborator who can make changes outside CourseFoundry and you can sync them via a commit.

How do I delete my account?

Account deletion is currently handled by contacting support. Send a request to support@coursefoundry.com and your account, courses, and data will be deleted within 30 days in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
